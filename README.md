1️⃣ Project Title / Headline
Walmart Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Walmart’s sales trends, store performance, and the impact of economic and seasonal factors on revenue.

2️⃣ Short Description / Purpose

This dashboard provides a data-driven overview of Walmart’s sales performance across stores and time periods. It evaluates how external factors such as holidays, fuel prices, CPI, unemployment, and temperature influence overall sales performance.

The objective is to help decision-makers understand revenue patterns, identify performance fluctuations, and optimize operational strategy using real business insights.

3️⃣ Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Data visualization and reporting

🔄 Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation

🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Custom KPIs and measures

🗂 Data Modeling – Relationship building across sales and economic indicators

📁 File Format – .pbix (development) and .png (presentation preview)

4️⃣ Data Source

Source: Walmart Sales Dataset (Retail + Economic Indicators Dataset)

The dataset includes:

Date

Store

Weekly Sales

Holiday Flag

Temperature

Fuel Price

Consumer Price Index (CPI)

Unemployment Rate

The data is structured at weekly transaction-level granularity, enabling time-series and factor-based analysis.

5️⃣ Features / Highlights (Business-Focused Explanation)

This section is critical. Interviewers don’t care about visuals — they care about business reasoning.

🔹 Business Problem

Retail chains like Walmart operate in dynamic environments influenced by:

Seasonal demand fluctuations

Economic conditions

Fuel price volatility

Consumer purchasing power

Holiday-driven sales spikes

However, management needs clear answers to:

Which store generates the highest revenue?

Do holidays significantly increase sales?

Does fuel price impact customer spending?

How does CPI affect purchasing trends?

Is unemployment influencing overall demand?

Is temperature correlated with sales variation?

Raw weekly data cannot provide quick strategic insights.

🔹 Goal of the Dashboard

To create a centralized analytical tool that:

Tracks overall sales performance

Monitors economic factor impact on revenue

Identifies store-level performance differences

Analyzes seasonal & holiday effects

Supports pricing, stocking, and operational decisions
